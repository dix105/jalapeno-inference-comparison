How to read this

  1. Jalapeño’s only published performance number is 1.5–1.9× work per watt vs GPUs on SemiAnalysis InferenceX. There is no tok/s. This page does not invent one.
  2. Speed bars are not apples-to-apples unless you pick a single model. Gemma 4 31B at 3,400 tok/s is a different job than GPT OSS 120B at 500.
  3. Where a vendor and Artificial Analysis disagree (Cerebras WSE-3 on GPT OSS 120B: 3,000 official vs 1,715 measured), both are shown.
  4. Cost here is implied energy for the same work from the announced multiplier — not an API price. OpenAI has not priced Jalapeño. No $/Mtok is fabricated.

What is missing

H100, H200, and B200 have no single headline tok/s in this dataset. GPU throughput is model-, batch-, and stack-dependent. They appear as the GPU class OpenAI compared against, not as fake scores.

Cerebras, Groq, and Groq 3 LPX have no InferenceX work-per-watt figure in the Jalapeño release, so they stay blank on the power view.
